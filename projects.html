Interpretable AI for Clinical Decision Support

NSF CAREER Award | 2024-2029

This project aims to develop interpretable machine learning systems that can assist clinicians in making complex diagnostic and treatment decisions. We are building novel neural architectures that provide human-understandable explanations while maintaining high predictive accuracy. The project involves close collaboration with medical professionals at three major hospitals.

Key innovations include: (1) prototype-based neural networks that reference similar historical cases, (2) attention mechanisms that highlight relevant features in medical images and patient records, and (3) interactive visualization tools that allow clinicians to explore and validate model predictions.

Human-AI Collaborative Decision Making

In collaboration with Industry Partner | 2023-Present

Investigating how humans and AI systems can effectively work together in high-stakes decision-making scenarios. We conduct user studies, develop interaction paradigms, and create computational models of human-AI collaboration. The project spans applications in healthcare, finance, and legal domains.

Robust and Fair Machine Learning

Grant Support from XYZ Foundation | 2023-2025

Developing techniques to build ML models that are simultaneously robust to adversarial attacks and fair across different demographic groups. We explore the inherent trade-offs between robustness and fairness, and propose methods to optimize both objectives jointly. The work has implications for deploying ML in sensitive domains.

Neural Dialogue Systems with Controllable Attributes

PhD Thesis Project | 2018-2022

My doctoral research focused on building dialogue systems that can flexibly control conversational attributes like persona, emotion, and style. I developed novel neural architectures and training procedures that enable fine-grained control while maintaining conversation quality. The work resulted in multiple publications and open-source releases.

Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning

Collaboration with Tech Company | 2021-2022

Developed novel federated learning protocols with formal differential privacy guarantees. The work addressed key challenges in distributed learning including communication efficiency, model convergence, and privacy-utility trade-offs. Our methods were evaluated on large-scale real-world datasets.
